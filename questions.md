# Car Insurance Intake Questions (Ireland)

Use this as the master checklist for any new user. Add insurer-specific extras as we discover them.

## Customer

- What is your title? (Mr/Mrs/Ms/Miss/Dr)
- What is your first name?
- What is your last name?
- What is your date of birth? (DD/MM/YYYY)
- What is your sex/gender? (if required by insurer)
- What is your main occupation / employment status?
- What is your phone number?
- What is your email address?

## Address

- What is your Eircode?
- What is your full address (Address line 1, line 2, town/city, county)?
- Is the car kept overnight at this address? If not, where is it kept?
- Is your postal address the same as your home/overnight address?
- Sub-area / locality within the county (some insurers require this, e.g. Ashbourne within Co. Meath)
- If the insurer finds or suggests an address, should we confirm it as the correct billing/home address?

## Residency (some insurers)

- How long have you been a permanent resident in the Republic of Ireland?

## Licence / Driving

- What type of licence do you hold? (Full Irish / Irish learner permit / Full UK / Full EU / Other)
- How many years have you held this licence?
- Did you pass your driving test in Ireland?
- How many years have you been driving in Ireland? (may be exact years or a band)

## Vehicle

- Vehicle registration number?
- If reg lookup fails: make/model/trim/engine/fuel/body type (whatever the insurer asks)
- Are you the main driver?
- Are you the registered owner?
- When did you buy the car? (month/year or exact date)
- What is the car worth in today's market?
- How many kilometres/miles will you drive in the next 12 months? (exact or band)
- What is the vehicle use? (Social, domestic & pleasure vs commuting/business)

## Cover

- When should the policy start? (DD/MM/YYYY)
- What cover types do you want prices for?
- Comprehensive
- Third Party Fire & Theft
- Third Party Only

## Insurance History / NCB

- What type of insurance have you had most recently? (own name / named driver / company car / none)
- Have you had a policy in your own name in the last 2 years?
- No Claims Bonus / No Claims Discount years (and whether it is currently in use on another policy)
- Consecutive claims-free years as a named driver (if applicable)
- Which country was your NCB earned in?

## Claims / Convictions

- Any penalty points in the last 3 years?
- Any convictions/offences in the last 3 years?
- Any accidents or claims (including non-fault) in the last 3–4 years?

## Additional Drivers

- Any additional drivers to add? If yes, collect their details (DOB, licence, history, etc.)

## Declarations / Consent

- Accept terms of business / disclosure requirements / data protection statement
- Marketing/renewal contact consent (if asked)

## Notes We’ve Seen So Far

- Some sites validate phone numbers strictly (digits only, must start with 0, length constraints).
- Some sites require county sub-area selection in addition to county.
- Anti-bot challenges can appear mid-flow (manual solve required).
